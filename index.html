01 — About the Company

Who We Are

AurinkoTech UG (limited liability) is a technology company based in Bavaria, Germany. We bring together hardware engineering and software development under one roof, sourcing and building hardware while developing our own software products.

Mission

Make good technology easy to reach

We connect the gap between capable hardware and the software that runs on it, so that dependable technology is never out of reach.

Vision

A rising standard for both worlds

We see hardware and software as one continuous product experience, not two separate disciplines — and we build accordingly.

Business purpose: the distribution and development of hardware and software (Vertrieb und Entwicklung von Hard- und Software), operated as a UG (limited liability) under German law.

03 — What We Do

Hardware & Software, Under One Roof

Hardware Distribution & Development

We source, supply and develop dependable technology hardware, matched to the systems it needs to run.

  • Technology hardware sourcing & distribution
  • Hardware development & integration
  • Deployment-ready configuration

Software Development

We are not a service provider — we build and own our software. Every product is developed in-house, end to end.

  • Proprietary software products, built in-house
  • Full ownership from concept to release
  • Continuous development and iteration

Our model: two disciplines, one company. AurinkoTech distributes and develops hardware while developing our own software products — not software-as-a-service work for hire, but technology we design, build and stand behind ourselves.

04 — Our Developments

What We've Built
